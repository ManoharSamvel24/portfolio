I'm a Computer Science graduate with a deep curiosity for how software works beneath the surface. What started as an interest in computers grew into a passion for building reliable applications, solving real problems, and continuously learning the craft of software development.

Today, I work as a mobile app developer at Icomm Technologies, where I develop FormulaHR's Android and iOS apps end-to-end — from design and architecture to backend integration and production release. Java has been at the core of my development journey, giving me a strong foundation in object-oriented design, clean architecture, and maintainable software engineering practices. Beyond mobile development, I enjoy working with backend systems, APIs, and the technical decisions that make software scalable, stable, and easy to maintain.

I care deeply about writing clean code, creating intuitive user experiences, and building software that people can depend on. For me, great development is not just about implementing requirements—it's about understanding the problem, thinking from the user's perspective, and delivering solutions that provide lasting value.

When I'm away from the keyboard, I enjoy reading Sherlock Holmes mysteries, learning new technologies, and spending time with Scripture. These interests continue to shape my curiosity, discipline, and approach to problem-solving.
